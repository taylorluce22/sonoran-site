Supplier FAQ

Questions facilities ask.

08
How are you paid?+

By the facilities we work with, and only on results. No retainer, no upfront fee. The specific structure is agreed on the first call and put in writing before any introduction is made.

Are you a distributor or a broker?+

Neither. We never take title, never hold inventory, never transmit pricing, and never process an order. We are a marketing and introduction agency — you remain seller-of-record on every transaction.

Who owns the customer relationship?+

You do, from the first conversation. You quote, contract, invoice and ship. We never sit between you and the buyer.

What does “license-verified” actually mean?+

Licensure confirmed at the issuing state board — the primary source, not a directory listing. The medical director confirmed. The buyer’s state matched against your ship-to map. The evidence file arrives with the introduction.

What happens when a buyer can’t be verified?+

They aren’t introduced. There is no second tier.

What happens if an introduced buyer never orders?+

Then we aren’t paid. The risk of a weak introduction sits with us — that’s why the verification standard exists.

Do you work with our competitors?+

We work with a small number of non-competing facilities per category. If your category is already covered, we’ll say so on the first call.

What do you need from us to start?+

A 20-minute call, your category list, and your ship-to map. That’s enough to know whether the fit is real.

What if we want to stop?+

Say so. There’s no exclusivity requirement, and the terms for ending the relationship are set out in plain language in the agreement you sign before the first introduction.

Where do you operate?+

We’re founded in Scottsdale, Arizona, and work nationally — buyer segments are matched to the states where each facility is licensed to ship.
